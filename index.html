01Profile

I'm a clinician-scientist working at the intersection of chronobiology, metabolic physiology, and systemic health. For over a decade I've studied how circadian disruption during pregnancy (chronodisruption) programs metabolic and cardiovascular disease in the offspring, and how maternal melatonin can prevent it in animal models. This work has been published in journals such as The Journal of Physiology, Journal of Pineal Research, and Frontiers in Endocrinology.

Trained as a dentist and now Head of the Department of Dentistry, I connect oral and systemic health through the perio-systemic axis, the biological link between periodontal disease, obesity, and adipose-tissue inflammation.

In parallel, I build and deploy the software that supports this work, from automated assessment tools and reproducible-research platforms to the Linux infrastructure that runs them, bringing engineering rigor to academic and clinical workflows.

02Research

My work follows three connected lines, from the lab bench to the clinic.

Chronodisruption & developmental programming

My main line, and my FONDECYT project as principal investigator: how disrupted light–dark cycles during pregnancy program metabolic, endocrine and cardiovascular disease in the offspring, and whether maternal melatonin prevents it in rat models.

Key papers

  • Halabi et al., Front Endocrinol 2021
  • Salazar et al., J Physiol 2018
  • Mendez et al., J Pineal Res 2023

The perio-systemic axis

How periodontitis and obesity feed each other through adipose-tissue inflammation, studied from clinical populations to the lab, including the effect of periodontitis-derived exosomes on adipose tissue.

Key papers

  • Juárez-Membreño et al., Rev Odontol Mex 2026
  • Maher et al., Int J Morphol 2023
  • Juárez et al., J Oral Res 2015

Clinical epidemiology & research methods

Risk-factor modelling, randomised trials and the methodological quality of dental research. This is the foundation of the Diploma in Clinical Epidemiology I will direct from 2027.

Key papers

  • Halabi et al., J Oral Maxillofac Surg 2012
  • Halabi et al., J Appl Oral Sci 2018
  • Ruiz et al., Odontoestomatología 2018
